Participant Information

Welcome to the delegation study! Please carefully read the information below before you decide to participate in this study.

Project Summary

The aim of this study is to examine how people make decisions when delegating work between themselves and automated systems in a warehouse task environment.

Time Commitment

The study typically takes 15 minutes to complete.

Benefits & Risks

There are no direct benefits from your participation in this study. There are no known risks associated with participation.

Eligibility Requirements

  • You can participate only once in this study
  • United States citizen/resident
  • 18 years or older
  • English speaker

Reimbursement & Compensation

You will receive $2.00 US in return for your participation.

Note: If your answers are below a minimal threshold for quality, you will not be compensated.

Confidentiality & Anonymity

All research data collected will be stored securely and confidentially on a password protected server indefinitely. Identifying details will not be associated with any data provided by you as part of any publication or presentation.
